How to Make Microwave Mug Brownie:

Here are the ingredients you would need to make a Microwave Mug Brownie

-Flour

-Sugar

-Cocoa Powder

-Melted Butter

-Milk

Here are the 5 steps you will need make Microwave Mug Brownie:

1. Grab a microwave-safe coffee mug.

2. Whisk the flour, sugar, and cocoa powder together inside the mug.

3. Pour in your melted butter and milk.

4. Stir with a fork until it turns into a smooth batter.

5. Microwave on high power for exactly 60 seconds.
