Here are the 5 ingredient's you will need to make a Microwave Mug Brownie:

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • cocoa powder
  • melted butter
  • milk

Here are the 5 steps you will need to make a Microwave Mug Brownie:

  • Grab a microwave-safe coffee mug.
  • Whisk the flour, sugar, and cocoa powder together inside the mug.
  • Pour in your melted butter and milk.
  • Stir with a fork until it turns into a smooth batter.
  • Microwave on high power for exactly 60 seconds.

And Now You have made a delicious microwave mug brownie!
