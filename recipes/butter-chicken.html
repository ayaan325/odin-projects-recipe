How to Make Butter Chicken

Here are the ingredients you would need to make Butter Chicken

-Chicken Breast

-Tomato-Sauce

-Heavy-Cream

-Butter

-Curry Powder

Here are the 5 steps you will need make butter chicken:

1. Cut the chicken into bite size cubes

2. Melt butter in a warm pan and brown the chicken

3. Pour in the tomato sauce and stir in the curry powder.

4. Pour in the heavy cream and turn the heat down low.

5. Let it simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce gets thick.
