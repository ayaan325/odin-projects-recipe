How to Make Grilled Cheese

Here are the ingredients you would need to make Grilled-Cheese

-Sliced Sourdough Bread

-Chedder Cheese

-Butter

-Garlic Powder

-Mayonaise

Here are the 5 steps you will need make Grilled-Cheese:

1. Mix your soft butter with a pinch of garlic powder.

2. Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on the inside of the bread slices.

3. Place your cheese slices between the mayo-sided bread pieces.

4. Spread your garlic butter on the outside faces of the sandwich.

5. Grill in a pan on medium heat until both sides are golden brown. Let it simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce gets thick.
