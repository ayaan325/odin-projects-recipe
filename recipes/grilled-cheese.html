Here are the 5 ingredient's you will need to make a grilled-cheese:

  • Sliced bread
  • cheddar cheese
  • butter
  • garlic powder
  • mayonnaise

Here are the 5 steps you will need to make a grilled-cheese:

  • Mix your soft butter with a pinch of garlic powder.
  • Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on the inside of the bread slices.
  • Place your cheese slices between the mayo-sided bread pieces.
  • Spread your garlic butter on the outside faces of the sandwich.
  • Grill in a pan on medium heat until both sides are golden brown.

And Now You have made a delicious Grilled Cheese!
