How to Make Banana Pancakes:

Here are the ingredients you would need to make Banana Pancakes

-1 ripe banana

-2 eggs

-Cinnamon

-Butter

-Maple Syrup

Here are the 5 steps you will need make Banana Pancakes:

Mash the banana completely in a bowl using a fork.

Crack the eggs into the bowl and whisk them into the banana.

Stir in a small pinch of cinnamon until combined.

Melt butter in a frying pan over medium-low heat.

Cook small scoops of batter for 2 minutes on each side and serve with syrup.
