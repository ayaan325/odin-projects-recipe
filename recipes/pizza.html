How to Make Pepperoni Pizza

Here are the ingredients you would need to make Pepperoni Pizza

-Pre-made pizza dough

-Pizza Sauce

-Shredded Mozzarella

-Pepperoni Slices

-Olive Oil

Here are the 5 steps you will need make Pepperoni Pizza:

1. Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).

2. Roll out your pizza dough flat onto a baking sheet.

3. Spread a thin layer of pizza sauce over the dough.

4. Sprinkle your mozzarella cheese and layer the pepperoni on top.

5. Brush the edges with olive oil and bake for 12 minutes.
