Here are the 5 ingredient's you will need to make butter chicken:

  • Chicken Breast
  • Tomato-Sauce
  • Heavy-Cream
  • Butter
  • Curry Powder

Here are the 5 steps you will need make butter chicken:

  • Cut the chicken into bite size cubes
  • Melt butter in a warm pan and brown the chicken
  • Pour in the tomato sauce and stir in the curry powder.
  • Pour in the heavy cream and turn the heat down low.
  • Let it simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce gets thick.

And Now You have made a delicious Butter Chicken!
