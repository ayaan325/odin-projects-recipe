Here are the 5 ingredient's you will need to make pepperoni pizza:

  • Pre-made pizza dough
  • Pizza Sauce
  • Shredded Mozzarella
  • Pepperoni Slices
  • Olive Oil

Here are the 5 steps you will need to make Pizza:

  • Preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C).
  • Roll out your pizza dough flat onto a baking sheet.
  • Spread a thin layer of pizza sauce over the dough.
  • Sprinkle your mozzarella cheese and layer the pepperoni on top.
  • Brush the edges with olive oil and bake for 12 minutes.

And Now You have made a delicious pepperoni pizza!
